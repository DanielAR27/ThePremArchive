How are these metrics computed?
  • Precision@10: relevant results divided by the results the person could judge (“can’t tell” answers are left out), averaged over the rated searches (Manning, Raghavan & Schütze, 2008, ch. 8).
  • MRR: the average of 1 ÷ the position of the first relevant result, or 0 if there is none (Voorhees, 1999).
  • nDCG@10: rewards relevant results that appear near the top, compared with the best possible order of the same results (Järvelin & Kekäläinen, 2002). It is computed over the results that were rated.
  • Information need met: from the question “did these results give you what you were looking for?”.
  • Cohen’s kappa: agreement between two people who rated the same result for the same search, corrected for chance (Cohen, 1960).
